## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

If you love dumplings but could happily skip the folding, this one’s for you. You get tender wrapper layers, a savory turkey filling packed with ginger and scallions, and a soy-sesame finish that makes every spoonful even better.

It’s cozy, filling and made for scooping straight into bowls. Think soft dumpling layers rather than tidy lasagna slices. All that lovely filling stays right where you want it, with no individual dumplings to seal.

## Simple Ingredients

Ginger, garlic and scallions bring the flavor, while finely chopped mushrooms and shredded cabbage soften into the turkey. The wonton wrappers give you those tender layers without making dough from scratch.

The little detail that matters most is keeping the filling moist enough to soften the wrappers. Spoon the liquid right out to the edges as you build each layer so you don’t end up with dry corners. It takes a little care here, but it’s what makes the finished bake soft and scoopable.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Give the bake its brief rest, then scoop it into four bowls and share the finishing sauce and reserved scallion greens over the top. The sauce brings a little sweetness, tang and heat to the warm filling, so make sure each portion gets its share.

This also works for planned leftovers when you want a comforting dinner ready to reheat. Follow the storage and reheating notes below.

## A Few Helpful Tips

- Keep unused wrappers covered with a damp towel so they stay flexible.
- Spoon filling liquid over every wrapper edge, then cover the pan tightly. Dry corners need moisture, not more browning.
- Use a nonmetal utensil to lift a wrapper sample clear of a coated pan before checking its tenderness.
