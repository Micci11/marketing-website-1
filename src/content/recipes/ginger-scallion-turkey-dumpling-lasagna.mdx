## Before you begin

Use a 9-inch / 23 cm square baking dish at least 2 inches / 5 cm deep, plus foil or a tight-fitting oven-safe lid. The layer plan assumes thin square wrappers approximately 3–3½ inches / 7.5–9 cm across; measure yours before layering. Weigh meat and vegetables raw, after trimming, and wrappers as sold. Active and total times are estimates.

## Wrapper notes and allergens

Contains wheat, soy and sesame with the reference products. Some wrapper brands also contain egg; check the package. Do not substitute rice paper, egg-roll wrappers or thick dumpling skins without a separate hydration test.
