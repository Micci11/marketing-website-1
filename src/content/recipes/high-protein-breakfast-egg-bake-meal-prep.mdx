## Six Individual Breakfast Portions

Set out six oven-safe containers or mold cavities. The base for each portion is one egg, half a cup of egg whites and half a cup of cottage cheese.

## Choose Your Add-Ins

The vegetables, fully cooked sausage and shredded cheese are optional. If using all three, divide the listed amounts evenly: half a cup of vegetables, one sliced sausage link and one label-defined serving of cheese per container. Check the cheese package for its serving weight.

## Cook Before Storing

Stir each portion, support the containers on a baking sheet and check the centers for doneness. Fully cook every portion before refrigerating or freezing; use the temperatures and storage directions in the recipe card.

The nutrition figures have not been verified for each combination of add-ins. Use the labels on your ingredients when calculating your own batch.
