## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

Sweet, spicy, smoky and creamy all in one bowl. The beef brings the savory flavor, the sweet potato gets those lovely browned edges, and the cottage cheese keeps everything cool and creamy underneath. Then comes the hot honey. That little drizzle brings the whole thing together.

The lime-soaked onion adds just enough crunch and tang to keep you going back for another bite. And while the potatoes roast, you can get the beef and toppings ready, so dinner comes together without juggling everything at once.

## Simple Ingredients

This is all about getting a little of everything in each spoonful. Smoked paprika and cumin give the beef plenty of flavor, while honey and sweet potato balance the heat from the hot sauce. The cottage cheese makes a creamy base without another dressing to mix.

Keep the cottage cheese cold until you build the bowls. Warm beef and golden potato against that cool, creamy base is such a good combination, and the lime and fresh cilantro finish it off beautifully.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Serve while the beef and sweet potato are warm, with the onion, hot honey and cilantro shared between both bowls. Get your fork right down to the cottage cheese so you get all those flavors together.

Making it ahead? Keep the beef and potato separate from the cottage cheese and fresh toppings, then build your bowl after reheating. The storage notes below have the timing and reheating details.

## Before you begin

Weigh the beef and vegetables raw, after trimming. Divide the finished recipe evenly into two bowls.

## A Few Helpful Tips

- Cut the sweet potato into even ½–⅝-inch (1.5 cm) cubes so it cooks evenly. Give it room on the tray for browned edges.
- Leave the onion in the lime juice while the potatoes roast, and keep that tangy liquid for the bowls.
- Keep the cottage cheese chilled until serving for a cool contrast to the beef and potato.
