## Why You'll Love This Recipe

This bowl has a little bit of everything: warm, smoky beef, sweet potato with browned edges, cool cottage cheese, and a sticky hot-honey finish. The lime-soaked onion brings the sharp crunch that keeps each bite interesting.

Once the potatoes go into the oven, you have time to make the toppings and cook the beef. Then everything comes together in two generous bowls.

## Simple Ingredients

The sweet potato and honey bring sweetness, while the hot sauce and lime balance it out. Cottage cheese makes a creamy base without needing a separate dressing. Keep it cold until you're ready to build the bowls so you get that warm-and-cool contrast.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Serve as soon as the beef and potatoes are ready, with a little of each topping in every bowl. For meal prep, keep the warm components separate from the cottage cheese and fresh toppings, then assemble after reheating. The storage notes below cover the timing.

## Before you begin

Weigh the beef and vegetables raw, after trimming. Divide the finished recipe evenly into two bowls. Active and total times are estimates.

## Allergens

Contains milk. Check sauce labels for any additional allergens.
