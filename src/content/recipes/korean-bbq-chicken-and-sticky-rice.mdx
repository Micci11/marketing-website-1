## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

The gochujang gives the chicken a savory kick, and the honey adds a little sweetness to balance it. Add sticky rice and a few fresh toppings, and every forkful gets a little of everything.

The recipe makes four portions, so it’s a useful one to cook when you want dinner and a few lunches from the same batch.

## Simple Ingredients

Chicken thighs, gochujang, honey and Mingle Sweet & Spicy Korean BBQ seasoning do most of the work here. A similar Korean-style garlic, ginger and chilli seasoning is an option too; start with a lighter hand if yours is saltier, then adjust to taste. The rice gets rice vinegar, salt, and either sweetener or sugar.

Weigh the sushi or sticky rice while it’s dry. Cook it first, stir in the seasoning, and give it the ten-minute rest in the recipe below.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Cucumber is lovely alongside the sweet-and-spicy chicken if you want some crunch. You can also add steamed pak choy, spring onion, black sesame and a little teriyaki drizzle. Choose the extras you enjoy; you don’t need to make a production of it.

## A Few Helpful Tips

- Spread the chicken out on the tray and turn it halfway through cooking.
- Gochujang brings the heat, so choose a variety you enjoy. Keep the recipe’s measured amount the same.
- The chicken cooks for 20–25 minutes at 200°C / about 400°F. Check it reaches 74°C / 165°F in the thickest part before serving.
- Sugar and sweetener are both options for the rice, but they change the nutrition estimate.
