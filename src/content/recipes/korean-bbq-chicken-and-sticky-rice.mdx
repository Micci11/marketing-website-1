## Why You'll Love This Recipe

I made this recipe exactly as written and liked it. Chicken thighs, gochujang and honey make a flavorful pairing, and the sticky rice gives the sauce somewhere to go.

## Simple Ingredients

The chicken uses Mingle Sweet & Spicy Korean BBQ seasoning alongside the gochujang and honey. The rice gets rice vinegar, salt and either sweetener or sugar, just as the source allows.

Weigh the **250 g of rice dry**. The cooked yield depends on the rice and its package directions, so the recipe keeps the original dry weight.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Serve the chicken over the seasoned rice. Cucumber, steamed pak choy, a teriyaki drizzle, black sesame and spring onion are the source's optional additions. Pick the ones you want without treating them as part of a fixed nutrition calculation.

## Before you begin

Allow 20–25 minutes for the chicken and a 10-minute rest for the seasoned rice. The oven setting of 400°F is an approximate equivalent of 200°C. Check the chicken's internal temperature before serving.

Use the specified seasoning to reproduce the tested version. The amount is to taste, and the cooking spray and toppings have no fixed quantities. The US view uses weight equivalents for gochujang, honey, rice and sweetener so it does not assume they all pack into a measuring cup the same way.

## Ingredient notes and allergens

Gochujang and teriyaki can contain soy and wheat; check the brands you use. Sesame is an optional topping. Sweetener and sugar are both source options, but they do not have identical nutrition.
