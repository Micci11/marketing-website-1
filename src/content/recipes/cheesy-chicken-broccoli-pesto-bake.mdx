## Why this recipe works

This makes four substantial portions with very little hands-on work. Mix, cover tightly and let the oven get on with it. The final uncovered bake gives you that golden cheese top, with creamy pesto rice waiting underneath.

The useful detail is the foil seal. Rice needs moisture and trapped steam to soften, so a loose sheet sitting on top won’t do the same job. Keep the 7.1 oz / 200 g dry rice below the liquid before you cover the dish.

## Getting the ingredients right

Use uncooked basmati rice, not a pouch of ready-cooked rice. Rinse it, then drain well so you aren’t adding lots of unmeasured water. Cut the 1 lb 5.2 oz / 600 g chicken into similar-sized pieces and chop the broccoli small enough to distribute evenly.

Basil pesto brings most of the flavor. Choose one you enjoy, and check the label for nuts and cheese if you are cooking around allergies. Thick, plain Greek yogurt is the right match here; save sweetened yogurt for another recipe.

## Tools worth having

A large, deep oven-safe baking dish and a roll of foil are the main essentials. Leave enough room to mix without spilling and seal the foil firmly around the rim. An instant-read thermometer is the most useful extra: it checks that the chicken and center of the casserole have reached 165°F / 74°C without relying on the cheese’s color.

A deep 9 x 13-inch / 23 x 33 cm dish of about 5 US quarts / 4.7 liters is a practical size to consider for this bulky mixture, although the fit and timing depend on your dish. If you need a thermometer, the [ThermoWorks ThermoPop 2](https://www.thermoworks.com/products/thermopop-2) is one straightforward option. An existing reliable food thermometer does the same essential job.

For meal prep, choose shallow containers that hold one quarter of the bake. Glass or sturdy food-storage plastic are both fine; follow the container’s heating instructions.
