## A 10-Minute Cottage Cheese Breakfast

Cottage cheese forms the creamy base for these fried eggs, with chili oil for heat. The recipe takes 5 minutes of prep and 5 minutes of cooking for one serving.

## Keep It Spicy or Make It Mild

For a milder breakfast, reduce or skip the chili oil. Shallot, chives and extra chili oil are optional additions.

## Cooking Tips

Use a skillet with a lid. Cook over steady heat and cover the pan to help the egg whites set. Serve straight away, with toast or a tortilla if you like.
