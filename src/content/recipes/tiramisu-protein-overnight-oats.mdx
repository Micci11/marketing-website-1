## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

Coffee, vanilla cream and a little cocoa on top. These oats have that lovely tiramisu flavor in a breakfast you can make the night before. The coffee-soaked oats turn soft in the fridge, while the creamy topping makes the whole thing feel a little more special than your usual breakfast.

Cottage cheese and Greek yogurt bring the protein, so there’s no powder to buy or stir in. Make the two portions before bed, let the fridge do its part, and tomorrow’s breakfast is already waiting. Just add the cocoa and grab a spoon.

## Simple Ingredients

The rolled oats soften in cooled coffee and yogurt, with a little maple syrup and vanilla to round out the flavor. For the topping, the cottage cheese gets blended with the remaining yogurt until it’s smooth and creamy.

That quick blend is worth it. You want a soft vanilla layer to spoon through with the coffee oats, rather than little cottage cheese curds in each bite. The cocoa goes on just before eating for that familiar tiramisu finish.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Take your spoon through both layers so you get the creamy topping with the coffee oats. They’re especially good together. Add the cocoa just before eating, and enjoy both portions within two days, following the storage notes below.

If you prefer decaf, that works here too. You still get the coffee flavor and the same make-ahead breakfast.

## Before you begin

Makes two generous portions, approximately 12.3 oz / 350 g each. Have your brewed coffee cooled before mixing, and allow at least 8 hours in the fridge for the oats to soften.

## A Few Helpful Tips

- Cool the brewed coffee before mixing so the dairy stays cold.
- Scrape down the blender as you make the topping; stop when the cottage cheese cream is smooth.
- Wait until just before eating to dust on the cocoa.
