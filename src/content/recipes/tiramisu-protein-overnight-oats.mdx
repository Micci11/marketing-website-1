## Why You'll Love This Recipe

This is for mornings when coffee and something creamy sound like a good start. You get a coffee-soaked oat layer, a smooth vanilla cream, and cocoa on top, all waiting in the fridge.

The cottage cheese and Greek yogurt do the work in the cream layer, so there's no protein powder to buy. Make the two portions the night before, then add the cocoa when you're ready to eat.

## Simple Ingredients

Old-fashioned rolled oats soften overnight in the cooled coffee and yogurt. Blending the cottage cheese with the rest of the yogurt gives the topping its smooth texture. A little maple syrup and vanilla round out the coffee and cocoa.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Take your spoon through both layers so you get some cream with the coffee oats. Save the cocoa for the last moment, and plan to enjoy both portions within two days. Decaf works if you want the coffee flavor without regular coffee.

## Before you begin

Makes two generous jars, approximately 12.3 oz / 350 g each. The estimated total time includes at least 8 hours in the refrigerator and assumes the brewed coffee has already cooled. Active time is also an estimate.

## Ingredient notes and allergens

Contains milk. The reference oats are not certified gluten-free. Decaf coffee is a straightforward option. Do not substitute the same volume of undiluted espresso unless a much stronger flavor is wanted. No additional salt is included because the cottage cheese already contributes it.

## Tools That Make This Turn Out Right

This is a small batch, so keep the setup simple. A mini blender or small food processor you already own is a practical choice for the cream. Stop and scrape down as directed before deciding it needs more blending.
