## A few ingredients that make a difference

Finely grate the 7.1 oz / 200 g carrot rather than using chunky pre-shredded pieces. Smaller shreds spread through the batter more evenly and give you a softer bite.

The 10.6 oz / 300 g cottage cheese needs a good blend before the carrot goes in. For the frosting, light cream cheese brings the tang and 0% Greek yogurt keeps the mixture spreadable. Choose thick yogurt rather than a runny one.

Sweeteners vary a lot. Use one labeled for baking and follow its guidance for sweetness; this recipe leaves the amount to your taste. Taste the egg-free frosting as you go, but don’t taste raw cake batter.
