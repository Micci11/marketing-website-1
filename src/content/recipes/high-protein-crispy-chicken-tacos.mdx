  - "Cook for 6–8 minutes until golden, fully cooked, and slightly crispy on the edges."
  - "Remove from heat and set aside."
  - "In a bowl, mix the yogurt, light mayo, taco seasoning, and a dash of milk until smooth."
  - "Lay out the mini tacos and lightly rub one side with the chicken juices for extra flavor."

## Why You'll Love This Recipe

These tacos feel like a proper comfort meal. Crispy edges, melty cheese, juicy chicken, and a sauce that ties everything together. They also land at 25g of protein per serving, which makes them easy to fit into the day without any real compromise.

## How It Works

The layering is what makes this recipe.

Chicken thighs stay juicy under heat and get just enough color in the pan to give you that slightly crispy edge. The mozzarella melts around the filling and helps seal the taco when it hits the air fryer or oven. The yogurt-based sauce keeps things light without losing the richness you want with tacos.

Every component is doing something. That's why it works.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Serve hot with the sauce on the side, a squeeze of lime, and extra cilantro if you want it. This is the kind of meal that feels way more indulgent than the macros suggest, which is exactly the point.

- the pan isn't holding heat properly
- or the surface is sticking before the sear can develop

Fix that, and the whole recipe changes.
