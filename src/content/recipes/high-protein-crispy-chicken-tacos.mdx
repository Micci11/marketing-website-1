## Crispy Chicken Tacos in 35 Minutes

Seasoned chicken thighs, melted mozzarella and a yogurt-based sauce fill six mini tacos. Cook the chicken in a pan first, then finish the assembled tacos in the air fryer or oven.

## What Goes in the Tacos

The tortillas and mozzarella are required: the tortillas hold the filling, and the cheese helps the tacos stay together. Fresh salsa, cilantro, parmesan and lime are optional additions.

## Serving the Batch

The batch makes six mini tacos, with one taco per serving. Serve the creamy sauce alongside and add your preferred toppings.
