  - "2/3 cup low-fat yogurt"
  - "3 tbsp light mayo"
  - "Dash of milk (to thin sauce)"

optionalIngredients:
  - "6 mini street-style soft tacos"
  - "1/2 cup shredded mozzarella"
  - "Fresh salsa (tomato, red onion, cilantro, lime juice)"
  - "Chopped cilantro, for garnish"
  - "Grated parmesan, for garnish"
  - "Lime wedges, for serving"

instructions:
  - "Heat a pan over medium-high heat and lightly coat with cooking spray or butter."
  - "Add the chicken, olive oil, and taco seasoning."
  - "Cook for 6–8 minutes until golden, fully cooked, and slightly crispy on the edges."
  - "Remove from heat and set aside."
  - "In a bowl, mix the yogurt, light mayo, taco seasoning, and a dash of milk until smooth."
  - "Lay out the mini tacos and lightly rub one side with the chicken juices for extra flavor."
  - "Add mozzarella to each taco, followed by the cooked chicken and fresh salsa."
  - "Fold the tacos gently to hold the filling."
  - "Place in an oven or air fryer at 400°F / 200°C."
  - "Cook for 8–10 minutes, flipping halfway, until crispy and golden."
  - "Serve hot with the creamy sauce, garnish, and a squeeze of lime."

tips:

  - "Flip the tacos halfway through cooking for even crispiness."

  - "Getting the chicken slightly crispy before assembling is what makes the final texture work."
