## A Fully Cooked Chicken Shortcut

Start with 12 to 14 ounces of fully cooked chicken tenders and one-third of a cup of sauce. Reheat the tenders using their package directions, cut them into bites and toss with the sauce. Divide the finished batch into two servings.

## One Sauce for the Batch

Buffalo, BBQ and sweet chili are three alternatives for the same measured sauce amount. Ranch or blue cheese is an optional dip. Different sauces and tender brands change the nutrition, so check their labels.

## Keep the Breading from Sitting in Sauce

Reheat the chicken before adding sauce and toss it just before eating. If you are saving a portion, store the chicken and sauce separately where possible. The package instructions determine reheating time; there is no single temperature or timing that applies to every product.
