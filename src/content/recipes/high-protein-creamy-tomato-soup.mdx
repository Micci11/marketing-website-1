  - "Garlic powder, to taste"
  - "Onion powder, to taste"
  - "Mixed herbs, to taste"
  - "Salt and pepper, to taste"
  - "1 1/2 tbsp cornflour"

ingredientsWet:
  - "1 cup marinara sauce"
  - "1 cup cottage cheese"
  - "1 cup broth"
  - "Splash of water (to mix with cornflour)"

optionalIngredients:
  - "Fresh chilli, chopped"

instructions:
  - "In a container or directly in your pot, combine the marinara sauce, cottage cheese, broth, garlic powder, onion powder, mixed herbs, salt, and pepper."
  - "Blend until completely smooth using an immersion blender or standard blender. Blending properly is what gives this a creamy texture instead of a grainy one."
  - "In a small bowl, mix the cornflour with a splash of water to create a slurry."
  - "Pour the blended mixture into a saucepan over medium heat."
  - "Add the cornflour slurry and stir well."
  - "Cook for 5–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens and becomes smooth and creamy."
  - "Adjust seasoning to taste and serve hot."

tips:
  - "Blend thoroughly—this is what removes any cottage cheese texture."
  - "Add extra seasoning if needed—this recipe benefits from bold flavor."
  - "Stir consistently once heating to avoid lumps from the cornflour."
  - "If it thickens too much, add a splash of water or broth to loosen it."

variations:
  - "Add cooked ground turkey or chicken to turn it into a full pasta sauce."
  - "Use vegetable broth for a lighter flavor or chicken broth for more depth."
  - "Add parmesan on top for extra richness."

storage:
  - "Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days."
  - "Freeze in portions for longer storage and reheat as needed."

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

This sauce is one of those recipes that ends up getting used way more than you expect. It’s quick, high in protein, and actually feels like something you’d keep in your weekly rotation.

## Simple Ingredients

This works because of how everything blends together.

Cottage cheese gives you protein and creaminess, while marinara brings flavor. The key is getting it completely smooth before heating so it turns into a proper sauce instead of something grainy.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Use it over pasta, with chicken, as a dip, or as a base for other meals. It’s especially good when you make a batch and keep it ready for the week.
