## Tomato, Cottage Cheese and Broth

This recipe combines equal volumes of marinara, cottage cheese and broth. Blend the base, add the cornflour slurry and heat until thickened.

## A Smooth Base

Blend before heating, then stir as the mixture thickens. Taste and adjust the seasoning before serving warm. The recipe lists a 15-minute total time.
