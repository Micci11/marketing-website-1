ingredientsDry:
  - "Salt, to taste"
  - "Black pepper, to taste"
  - "1/4 cup breadcrumbs (optional, for coating)"
  - "2 tbsp grated parmesan"

ingredientsWet:
  - "11 oz chicken mince"
  - "1 large egg, beaten (optional, for coating)"

optionalIngredients:
  - "3 oz light mozzarella, cut into 4 sticks"
  - "Cooking spray"

instructions:
  - "Preheat the air fryer to 385°F / 195°C."
  - "Season the chicken mince with salt and black pepper and mix well."
  - "Divide the chicken mixture into 4 equal portions."
  - "Flatten one portion in your hand and place a mozzarella stick in the center."
  - "Wrap the chicken around the mozzarella and shape it into a fry-like stick."
  - "Repeat with the remaining chicken and mozzarella."
  - "For a crispier coating, dip each stick into beaten egg and roll in breadcrumbs mixed with parmesan."
  - "Lightly spray the coated chicken fries with cooking spray."
  - "Place the chicken fries in the air fryer in a single layer."
  - "Air fry for 10–15 minutes until golden brown and fully cooked through."
  - "Serve hot while the cheese is still melted inside."

tips:
  - "Seal the chicken tightly around the mozzarella so the cheese does not leak during cooking."
  - "Use the egg wash and breadcrumb coating if you want a crispier finish."
  - "Do not overcrowd the air fryer basket or the fries may steam instead of crisp."
  - "Keep the thickness consistent so they cook evenly and don’t split open."

variations:
  - "Use ground turkey instead of chicken if preferred."
  - "Add garlic powder, paprika, or chili flakes to the chicken mixture for more flavor."
  - "Skip the breadcrumbs for a lower-carb version."

storage:
  - "Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days."
  - "Reheat in the air fryer for a few minutes to bring back the crisp texture."

faq:
  - question: "Can I make these without breadcrumbs?"
    answer: "Yes. They will still cook well, but the outside will be less crispy."
  - question: "Why is the cheese leaking out?"
    answer: "Make sure the chicken fully seals around the mozzarella with no cracks or thin spots."
  - question: "Can I bake these instead?"
    answer: "Yes. Bake at 400°F / 200°C for about 18–20 minutes or until fully cooked through."

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

These hit that perfect combo of crispy outside and melted cheese inside, but still stay high in protein. They feel like something you’d order, but you can make them in under 30 minutes.

## Simple Ingredients

This works because of how the texture comes together.

Chicken forms the outer layer, mozzarella melts inside, and the optional coating gives that extra crisp. The key is keeping everything sealed and evenly shaped so it cooks properly.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Serve these hot with your favorite dip like marinara, ranch, or spicy mayo. They work as a snack, dinner, or something easy to prep ahead.
