## Cheese-Stuffed Chicken Sticks

These chicken fries are made from seasoned ground chicken wrapped around mozzarella sticks. There are no potatoes in the filling. The batch makes four chicken sticks, with one stick per serving.

## Required Filling, Optional Coating

The mozzarella is the filling and belongs in every stick. For a crispier coating, use the beaten egg, breadcrumbs and parmesan together. You can leave that coating off.

## Air Fryer Method

Seal the chicken around the cheese and keep the sticks evenly sized. Arrange them in a single layer so air can circulate, and check the chicken with a thermometer before serving.

Serve hot with your preferred dip, such as marinara, ranch or spicy mayo.
