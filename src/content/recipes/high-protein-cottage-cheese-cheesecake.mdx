  - "2 tbsp sweetener (or liquid sweetener like monk fruit)"

ingredientsWet:
  - "3 tbsp cottage cheese"
  - "2 oz cream cheese"
  - "1 large egg"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"

optionalIngredients:
  - "2 tbsp blueberries"

instructions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 325°F / 165°C."
  - "In a container or bowl, combine the cottage cheese, cream cheese, egg, sweetener, and vanilla extract."
  - "Blend until completely smooth using an immersion blender or standard blender. This step is what gives the cheesecake a smooth texture instead of a grainy one."
  - "Lightly grease a 6–8 oz ramekin or small baking dish."
  - "Pour the mixture into the dish."
  - "Add the blueberries on top and gently swirl them into the batter."
  - "Bake for 30–35 minutes until set but slightly soft in the center."
  - "Remove from the oven and let cool."
  - "Chill in the fridge for at least 2 hours before serving."

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

This is one of those low-effort recipes that actually delivers. It’s creamy, slightly sweet, and feels like dessert, but takes almost no time to throw together.

## Simple Ingredients

This works because of how simple it is, not in spite of it.

Cottage cheese gives you protein and structure, while cream cheese brings that classic richness. The texture mostly comes down to how smooth you blend it and what sweetener you use.

If you’re using liquid sweetener, something concentrated works best because it mixes smoothly and doesn’t affect the texture.

- Monk fruit drops (very concentrated, about 5 drops = 1 tsp sugar, so one bottle lasts a long time): https://amzn.to/4rTzAjq  

If you prefer a powdered option, this is the one I use now:

- Monk fruit + allulose blend: https://amzn.to/4uI6vdk  

I used to use monk fruit with erythritol, but it always gave me brain fog. This version is noticeably better and doesn’t have that after-effect.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Chill it fully and eat it straight from the fridge. That’s when it sets into that soft, creamy cheesecake texture.
