## Why this recipe works

Cooked shredded chicken keeps the preparation straightforward, and two rice-paper sheets per pocket give the filling a little more support. This CraveSmart version uses RedHot Buffalo sauce for that tangy, spicy kick.

You make three substantial hot pockets from one batch. The oven heats them through first, then a short turn under the broiler crisps the surface. Stay nearby for that last part: golden can become burnt surprisingly quickly.

## Get the filling and wrapper right

Weigh the chicken after cooking: you need 15.9 oz / 450 g cooked, shredded breast. The same weight of raw chicken will not give you the same amount of filling.

Mix in 2.7 US fl oz / 80 ml RedHot Buffalo sauce, along with cottage cheese, mozzarella and finely chopped green onion. Keep the shreds reasonably small so they’re easier to fold into a tidy pocket.

Dip the two rice-paper sheets together in beaten egg only until they begin to soften. They should bend without turning so floppy that they are difficult to move. A lightly oiled board helps stop them grabbing while you work.

## Tools worth having

A wide, shallow bowl makes dipping the rice paper easier than trying to bend it into a narrow cereal bowl. For cooking, use a metal tray that is safe under the broiler, with lightly greased foil as the liner. Ordinary parchment is not suitable under a broiler.

A broad spatula supports the filled pocket when you move it. An instant-read thermometer is also useful: you want the center hot, not just the outside browned. The [ThermoWorks ThermoPop 2](https://www.thermoworks.com/products/thermopop-2) is an optional example, but use a reliable thermometer you already own before buying another.
