## Why You'll Love This Recipe

I made the source recipe and really enjoyed it—even for breakfast. The sweet chilli glaze, lime and ginger give the chicken plenty of flavor, with sticky rice alongside to catch the sauce.

## Simple Ingredients

Chicken breast, an egg and cornflour make up the chicken portion. In US stores, look for **cornstarch** for the ingredient called cornflour here. Mingle Sweet Chilli Sauce, light soy sauce, garlic, ginger, lime and rice vinegar make up the glaze.

The **200 g rice is weighed dry**, before cooking. Keep that distinction when dividing the finished batch into four portions.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Build a bowl with the rice and glazed chicken, then finish with sesame and green onions. The source also suggests cucumber with soy sauce on the side. Those garnishes are optional and have no fixed quantities.

## Before you begin

Season the chicken **to taste**, using Mingle Use Me on Everything or salt and pepper. The coating order and gentle glaze simmer below expand the short original caption so you can follow the recipe without opening the video. No exact glaze reduction time is claimed.

This version uses **1 tablespoon = 15 mL** and **1 teaspoon = 5 mL**. The original sweet chilli caption does not define its spoons; these are CraveSmart's stated measuring standards. The source does not name a sweetener, so check your product's sweetness and start with less if it is concentrated.

## Ingredient notes and allergens

Contains egg and soy; check the soy sauce and seasoning labels for wheat and other allergens. Keep the named sweet chilli sauce when reproducing the tested recipe, since another sauce may change the sweetness and heat.
