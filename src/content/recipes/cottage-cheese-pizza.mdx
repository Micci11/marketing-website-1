optionalIngredients:

  - "1/2 cup pizza sauce"

instructions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 400°F / 200°C. Line a half sheet pan with parchment paper so the cheesy crust does not stick."
  - "In a bowl or blender, combine the cottage cheese and eggs and blend or whisk until mostly smooth. Blending gives you a smoother, more uniform crust."
  - "Add the flour, Italian seasoning, and garlic powder and mix until a thick batter forms."
  - "Spread the mixture evenly onto the lined half sheet pan to form a pizza base. Spread it thinner for a crispier crust or slightly thicker for a softer, breadier base."
  - "Bake for 15–20 minutes until the crust is set and lightly golden around the edges."
  - "Remove from the oven and spread the pizza sauce evenly over the crust."
  - "Top with shredded mozzarella and your desired toppings."
  - "Return to the oven and bake for another 8–10 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly."
  - "Let the pizza cool for 2–3 minutes, then slice with a sharp pizza cutter and serve hot."

tips:
  - "Blend the cottage cheese first if you want a smoother, more uniform crust texture."
  - "Spread the base thinly for a crispier, more traditional pizza style crust."
  - "Bake the crust until it is fully set and lightly golden before adding toppings to prevent sogginess."

## Why You Will Love This High-Protein Cottage Cheese Pizza

This is not diet pizza that tastes like a sad compromise. The crust bakes up like a cheesy, golden flatbread. The edges are crisp, the center is soft, and once you load it with sauce, mozzarella, and your favorite toppings, it eats like a real Friday night slice.

Each pan is loaded with protein, so you get that pizza night satisfaction without the heavy, sleepy crash afterward.

## Simple High-Protein Ingredients

The base uses cottage cheese and eggs to create a high protein crust instead of traditional dough. A small amount of flour gives it structure, and Italian seasoning plus garlic powder bring that classic pizzeria flavor.

You can keep it classic with mozzarella and pepperoni or turn it into a loaded macro friendly meal with lean protein and extra veggies.
