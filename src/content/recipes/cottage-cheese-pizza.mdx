instructions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 400°F / 200°C. Line a half sheet pan with parchment paper so the cheesy crust does not stick."
  - "In a bowl or blender, combine the cottage cheese and eggs and blend or whisk until mostly smooth. Blending gives you a smoother, more uniform crust."
  - "Add the flour, Italian seasoning, and garlic powder and mix until a thick batter forms."
  - "Spread the mixture evenly onto the lined half sheet pan to form a pizza base. Spread it thinner for a crispier crust or slightly thicker for a softer, breadier base."
  - "Bake for 15–20 minutes until the crust is set and lightly golden around the edges."
  - "Remove from the oven and spread the pizza sauce evenly over the crust."
  - "Top with shredded mozzarella and your desired toppings."
  - "Return to the oven and bake for another 8–10 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly."
  - "Let the pizza cool for 2–3 minutes, then slice with a sharp pizza cutter and serve hot."

## Why You'll Actually Love This
This is not diet pizza that tastes like a sad compromise. The crust bakes up like a cheesy, golden flatbread. The edges are crisp, the center is soft, and once you load it with sauce, mozzarella, and your favorite toppings, it tastes like Friday night pizza without the heavy, sleepy crash.

82g of protein per pan. That matters.

## The Ingredients (And Why They Work)
Cottage cheese and eggs are the backbone—they give you structure and protein without the dough. A small amount of flour keeps it from falling apart. Italian seasoning and garlic powder handle the flavor so it tastes like real pizza, not a gym experiment.

From there you're free. Keep it classic with mozzarella and pepperoni, or load it up with vegetables and lean protein. The crust holds either way.

## How to Serve It
Slice it 2-3 minutes out of the oven when the cheese is still melty and edges are crisp. Keep toppings simple for classic pizza vibes, or go loaded with vegetables and lean protein for a full, macro-friendly dinner.

Great for:

- High protein weeknight dinners  
- Meal prep, since it reheats well in the air fryer  
- A lighter alternative to takeout that still feels indulgent
