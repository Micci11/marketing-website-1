## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

These chicken and broccoli fritters get lovely golden edges in the pan, with melted mozzarella holding everything together. Garlic, smoked paprika and Italian herbs give them plenty of flavor without a long list of extras.

Serve them warm while the cheese is still soft. The batch makes eight large fritters, which is handy if you’re setting a few aside for lunch.

## Simple Ingredients

Chicken breast, broccoli, eggs and light mozzarella make up the base, with garlic powder, smoked paprika and Italian herbs for flavor. Chop the broccoli finely so it mixes evenly through the fritters.

Coles light mozzarella works here, but you don’t need to hunt down that exact brand. A similar shredded light mozzarella is a practical swap; check the label if you’re tracking macros.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Serve them warm with whatever side you fancy. They also portion nicely for lunches; keep any sides separate so you can reheat just the fritters.

## A Few Helpful Tips

- Dice the chicken and finely chop the broccoli before mixing.
- Start with a hot pan, then reduce it to medium-low when the fritters go in. This gives the centers time to cook through.
- Use a wide turner to support each fritter, and turn them 3–4 times as they cook.
- Check the center of each fritter reaches 74°C / 165°F. The outside can look ready before the chicken in the middle is done.
