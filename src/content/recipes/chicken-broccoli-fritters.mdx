## Why You'll Love This Recipe

I cooked these exactly as written and enjoyed them. The chicken stays in small pieces, the broccoli is finely chopped, and light mozzarella brings everything together into a savory fritter.

## Simple Ingredients

Chicken breast, broccoli, eggs and Coles light mozzarella form the base. Garlic powder, smoked paprika and Italian herbs add flavor without a separate sauce to make.

Keep the broccoli finely chopped as the source directs. The measured oil is **3 mL of avocado-oil spray**; a number of seconds spraying would not reliably reproduce that amount.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Enjoy the fritters warm and choose your own sides if you want a larger meal. The recipe makes **eight large fritters**, and the nutrition shown is **for one fritter**, not a full plate. No dip recipe or dip nutrition was supplied.

## Before you begin

Cooking time depends on the fritters' thickness and your pan. Turn them 3–4 times and check each center with a food thermometer. The chicken must reach 165°F / 74°C; a browned exterior alone does not show whether the middle is cooked.

A wide turner you already own is useful for supporting the fritters. Keep the source's diced chicken texture rather than turning the mixture into a puree.

## Ingredient notes and allergens

Contains egg and milk. Coles light mozzarella is the tested ingredient; a different cheese may behave differently and changes the creator's nutrition estimate.
