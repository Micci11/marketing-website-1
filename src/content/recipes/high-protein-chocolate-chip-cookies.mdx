## Why You'll Love This Recipe

These cookies have a soft center, crisp edges, and a real chocolate flavor. They taste like a treat, not a protein experiment.

## The Ingredient Science

Blend the cottage cheese with the vanilla and syrup before combining it with the other ingredients. The recipe lists one scoop of vanilla protein powder, but scoop weights vary by brand; check your product label when assessing a batch's nutrition.

## How to Serve Them

These are best once cooled so the structure sets right. If you want that softer center again later, a quick 20-second reheat brings it back without drying them out.
