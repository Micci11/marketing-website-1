instructions:
  - "Blend the cottage cheese, vanilla, and maple syrup until completely smooth."
  - "In a separate bowl, whisk the melted butter and sweetener together for about 1 minute."
  - "Add the egg and blended cottage cheese mixture, then mix for 30 seconds."
  - "Add the flour, protein powder, baking soda, baking powder, and salt. Mix until just combined."
  - "Fold in the chocolate chips."
  - "Chill the dough for 60 minutes. Skipping this step is the main reason cookies turn out flat instead of soft."
  - "Preheat the oven to 325°F / 165°C"
  - "Scoop into 10 cookies and place on a lined baking sheet."
  - "Bake for 16–18 minutes until lightly golden."

## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

These cookies have a soft center, crisp edges, and a real chocolate flavor without feeling like a “protein dessert.” They hit that balance where they actually feel like a treat, not a compromise.
