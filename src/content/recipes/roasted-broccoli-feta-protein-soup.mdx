## Why this recipe works

The oven does the flavor-building first. Broccoli, onion, garlic and feta roast together, then everything gets blended with broth, spinach and beans. You end up with about four generous bowls, depending on how much water you add at the end.

The soup is thick enough for a proper spoonful, but easy to loosen if you prefer it lighter. Add the lemon before the final seasoning check: that little bit of brightness makes a difference.

## A few ingredients that matter

Start with 5 cups of broccoli florets, roughly 14 oz / 400 g. That weight is a guide because small florets pack more tightly than large ones. Cut them into similar-sized pieces so they roast evenly.

The 6 oz / 170 g feta brings plenty of salt already, and broth varies by brand. Season lightly before roasting, then taste the blended soup before adding more. Plain cannellini beans give the soup body without a strong bean flavor.

## Tools worth having

A roomy roasting dish lets the broccoli brown rather than sit in a crowded pile. For blending, choose either a countertop blender specifically rated for hot liquids or an immersion blender used in a deep saucepan. A sealed smoothie cup is not the place for hot soup.

If you’re shopping for a blender, check its hot-liquid instructions and usable hot-fill limit, not just the advertised jug size. This is a substantial batch, so working in batches may still be necessary. A ladle and wide containers make portioning easier afterward.
