## Why You'll Love This Recipe

I made this recipe exactly as written and liked the combination of crisp cucumber, chicken and tangy yogurt dressing. It makes one serving and uses pulled chicken, so the assembly is straightforward once the chicken is ready.

## Simple Ingredients

Lebanese cucumber and natural Greek yogurt are the base. Garlic, lemon, wholegrain mustard and everything-bagel seasoning flavor the dressing. **MSG is included in the source recipe** and is retained here.

The source gives the MSG as 1 teaspoon, approximately 5 g. That is its stated estimate, not a new conversion for every brand.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Toss everything together and enjoy it as a chilled lunch, or eat it as soon as it is mixed. The source allows chilling but does not give a required time.

## Before you begin

Use **150 g of cooked, pulled chicken**, weighed after cooking, for this ready-to-eat salad. This is CraveSmart's explicit interpretation of the source's pulled-chicken quantity.

Choose plain, unsweetened natural Greek yogurt. The source does not specify a fat percentage, so use the kind you enjoy and expect the nutrition to vary. We mix the listed wholegrain mustard into the dressing to make the abbreviated method complete.

## Ingredient notes and allergens

Contains milk and mustard. Everything-bagel seasoning commonly includes sesame; check its label. Keep the seasoning and MSG quantities as supplied when recreating the tested recipe.
