## Why You’ll Love This Recipe

If you’ve got cooked chicken in the fridge, lunch is already halfway there. Add crisp cucumber and a creamy garlic-and-lemon dressing, give it a good toss, and you’ve got a generous bowl with plenty of crunch.

Wholegrain mustard and everything-bagel seasoning give the dressing a little extra flavor. It’s a small amount of prep for a very satisfying lunch.

## Simple Ingredients

Lebanese cucumber, cooked pulled chicken and plain natural Greek yogurt are the main ingredients. Garlic, lemon juice, wholegrain mustard and everything-bagel seasoning round out the dressing. Use any comparable everything-bagel blend you enjoy; check its salt level before adding more salt.

Weigh the chicken after cooking, and use plain, unsweetened yogurt so the dressing stays savory.

## Best Ways to Enjoy It

Eat it as soon as you’ve mixed it if you like the cucumber really crisp, or cover it and chill it for a cold lunch. Give it another toss before serving so you get a little dressing with every bite.

## A Few Helpful Tips

- Slice the cucumber and finely chop or grate the garlic before you start mixing.
- Use a bowl with enough room to toss everything without chasing cucumber over the edge.
- The yogurt you choose affects how rich the dressing is, as well as the nutrition estimate.
- If you’re cooking chicken for this salad, make sure it reaches 74°C / 165°F, then cool and refrigerate it safely before using it.
