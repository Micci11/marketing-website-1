High-Protein Chicken Calzone

A crispy, cheesy high-protein calzone made with chicken, cottage cheese, and low-fat mozzarella. Easy, filling, and seriously good.

Jump to Recipe 3/17/2026

Why You’ll Love This Recipe

This calzone hits that rare middle ground where it actually feels like comfort food but still fits your macros. You get a crispy, cheesy exterior and a hot, melty center without the heaviness that usually comes with it.

Simple Ingredients

The swaps here do more than just cut calories. They’re what make the texture work.

Cottage cheese keeps the inside soft and creamy, while chicken gives you structure and a much higher protein payoff. The parmesan coating is what creates that golden, slightly crisp shell on the outside.

It’s simple, but every piece has a job. That’s why this version actually works and doesn’t fall apart or turn bland.

Best Ways to Enjoy It

Serve it hot right after cooking while the outside is still crisp and the inside is melty. This is one of those meals that feels more like something you’d order than something you threw together in 25 minutes.

High-Protein Chicken Calzone

10 mins

15 mins

25 mins

2

9g

Ingredients

Switch between US and metric measurements. Metric conversions are approximate.

Dry Ingredients

  • 3.5 oz parmesan powder 99g parmesan powder

Wet Ingredients

  • 9 oz chicken mince 255g chicken mince
  • 2.8 oz chicken breast 79g chicken breast
  • 2 tbsp cottage cheese 30ml cottage cheese
  • 1/4 cup low-fat mozzarella 60ml low-fat mozzarella
  • 1 tomato, sliced 1 tomato, sliced
  • 2 tbsp extra low-fat mozzarella for the top 30ml extra low-fat mozzarella for the top

Optional

  • Light spray of olive oil or avocado oil Light spray of olive oil or avocado oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 385°F.
  2. Flatten the chicken mince into a calzone shape on parchment paper.
  3. Add the chicken breast, cottage cheese, low-fat mozzarella, and sliced tomato to one side.
  4. Fold the mince over and seal it closed to form a calzone.
  5. Lightly spray the outside with oil.
  6. Coat the outside fully with parmesan powder.
  7. Top with the extra low-fat mozzarella.
  8. Air fry for 15 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  9. Let rest for a few minutes before slicing and serving.

Tips

  • Seal the edges well so the filling stays inside while cooking.
  • A light oil spray helps the parmesan crisp up better.
  • Letting it rest briefly makes it easier to slice cleanly.
  • The air fryer gives the outside that crispy, golden crust while keeping the inside juicy.

Variations

  • Swap the tomato for spinach or roasted peppers.
  • Add Italian seasoning, garlic powder, or chili flakes to the chicken mince.
  • Use turkey mince for a slightly leaner version.

Storage

  • Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat in the air fryer for the best texture.

FAQ

Can I bake this instead of air frying?

Yes. Bake at 400°F until cooked through and golden, about 20 to 25 minutes.

Is this actually filling?

Yes. It is very high in protein and works well as a full meal.

Can I make it ahead?

Yes. Assemble it ahead of time and cook when ready.
